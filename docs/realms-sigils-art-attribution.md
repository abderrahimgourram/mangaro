# Realms’ Sigils artwork attribution

30 local fantasy relief illustrations for Mangaro’s «أختام العوالم».

## License and authors

The **foreground motifs** are by **Lorc** (http://lorcblog.blogspot.com) and
**Delapouite** (https://delapouite.com), published on **Game-icons.net**.
Each individual source page listed below was checked on **2026-10-08** and
explicitly credits the listed author under **Creative Commons Attribution 3.0
Unported (CC BY 3.0)**: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/ .
Commercial distribution and adaptation are permitted with attribution, a license
link and an indication of modifications. No non-commercial assets are included.
The untouched upstream license notice is packaged as `sigil-art/UPSTREAM-LICENSE.txt`.

**Modifications / original work by Mangaro:** six different fantasy frame
silhouettes, tier ornaments, metal/enamel shading, faceted stones, filigree,
engraving, distinct focal inlays and an original emerald imperial crown for
«الموقّر». These compositions remix the licensed motifs; they are not untouched
third-party illustrations. The original decorative artwork and resulting remix
illustrations are also supplied under CC BY 3.0; this does not change the
application’s code license. Do not imply endorsement by the original artists.

Attribution is available in-app from the collection toolbar and Settings → About
under **«فنون الأختام والتراخيص»**, with author links, license and all 30 source links. This document is also
packaged offline in the APK; artwork is never hotlinked/downloaded at runtime.

## Provenance and reproducibility

Official SVG repository: https://github.com/game-icons/icons .
Pinned upstream revision: `82d948812bfe3f269ef8f731dcdb07b08160edc4`.

`artwork/realms-sigils/manifest.json` records each source URL, author, license,
license verification date, source SVG SHA-256, license-page response SHA-256,
modifications, generated SVG/WebP SHA-256 and byte size. Original downloaded
sources are unchanged in `artwork/realms-sigils/sources/`; editable finished
illustrations are in `artwork/realms-sigils/illustrations/`.

Run `python3 tools/generate_sigil_art.py` with librsvg (`rsvg-convert`) and
ImageMagick (`magick`) installed to regenerate resources. No downloads occur;
source hashes are checked before rendering. `--id <stable-id>` rebuilds one item.
Output: 512×512 transparent WebP, quality 88, metadata stripped, local Android
`drawable-nodpi` resources. Coil uses bounded, shared resource decoding/cache;
large editable SVG originals are not shipped in the APK.

Artwork uses stylized engraved metal/vector relief, not copyrighted manhwa
panels or characters. Animations and rarity treatment are cosmetic only; existing
identifiers, Arabic names, objectives, progress, unlocks and XP stay unchanged.

## Research considered but not imported

- Game-icons.net official licensing documentation: https://game-icons.net/about.html
  and https://game-icons.net/faq.html (selected; direct author attribution).
- Ravenmore Fantasy Icon Pack: https://opengameart.org/content/fantasy-icon-pack-by-ravenmore-0
  (CC BY 3.0) and https://ravenmore.itch.io/fantasy-icon-pack . Attractive painted
  art, but insufficient matching motifs for one coherent 30-artifact collection.
- Kenney Retro Fantasy Kit: https://kenney.nl/assets/retro-fantasy-kit (CC0).
  Its retro 3D style does not match the intended metal/enamel artifact direction.
- 496 pixel-art RPG icons: https://opengameart.org/node/39455 (CC0, Henrique
  Lazarini / 7Soul1). Pixel art was not used for this collection.

No assets from these alternative collections, copyrighted panels, character art
or uncertain-license sources are included.
